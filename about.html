About & methodology

This site collects public, first-hand owner reports of how laptops run Omarchy and other Linux setups.

  • Every compatibility claim links to its source. We don't test hardware ourselves (yet).
  • “Unknown” means nobody has reported on it yet. It doesn't mean the feature is broken.
  • Overall status follows the report: Works, Works (minor fix), Experimental, or Problems.
  • Quotes are copied word for word. Reports are dated, and newer kernels and Omarchy releases can change results.
  • Corrections are welcome via the submit page.
