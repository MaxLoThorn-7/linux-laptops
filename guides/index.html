Buyer guides

Each guide collects the owner reports for one family of laptops, with sources. We haven't tested these machines.

  • Used ThinkPads on Omarchy: what owners report: Owner reports of Omarchy on seven ThinkPad models (T480, T490s, T14s Gen 3, T14 Gen 2, T470s, T450, X200), with sources and what is still unreported.
  • Old Intel MacBooks on Omarchy: what owners report: Owner reports of Omarchy on the 2015 Intel MacBook Pro and the 2016 15-inch Touch Bar MacBook Pro, with the reported T1 sound fix and sources.
  • Microsoft Surface on Omarchy: what owners report: Owner reports of Omarchy on Surface Laptop 3, Surface Pro 3, Surface Go, Surface Laptop 1st Gen and the experimental Surface Laptop 7 Snapdragon, with sources.
Every claim links to a public owner report. “unknown” means no report found yet, not “broken”.
