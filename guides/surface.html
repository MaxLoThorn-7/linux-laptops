Microsoft Surface on Omarchy: what owners report

These guides collect public owner reports. We haven't tested these machines ourselves. "Unknown" means nobody has reported on that part yet, not that it fails. A "works" report means one owner said it worked for them, not that every feature is confirmed. Reports are dated; newer releases can change results. Last reviewed: Nov 8, 2026.

Most Surface entries rest on one owner. Nobody in these reports has described the Type Cover keyboard or the pen.

Reading these reports

  • If you need the Type Cover or pen, no report here covers them.
  • The Surface Laptop 7 entry describes an in-development build, not a ready install.
  • Reports don't carry over to other Surface models or CPU variants.

General used-device checks (general advice, not from these reports)

  • Confirm the exact model and CPU/memory variant.
  • Ask about battery condition and whether a working charger is included.
  • If you need a keyboard, confirm the Type Cover is included.
Every claim links to a public owner report. “unknown” means no report found yet, not “broken”.
