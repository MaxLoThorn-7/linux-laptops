Used ThinkPads on Omarchy: what owners report

These guides collect public owner reports. We haven't tested these machines ourselves. "Unknown" means nobody has reported on that part yet, not that it fails. A "works" report means one owner said it worked for them, not that every feature is confirmed. Reports are dated; newer releases can change results. Last reviewed: Nov 8, 2026.

Reading these reports

  • A report covers the exact model the owner named. A T480 and a T480s are different laptops, and the T14 and T14s generations come in Intel and AMD versions. We don't carry a report over to a model or version nobody has reported on.
  • The T480 has the most reports here (three) and one documented quirk with dual batteries. Its Wi-Fi and suspend are still unreported.
  • The X200 report is from a pre-release build on an 18-year-old machine, so we mark it experimental.

General used-laptop checks (general advice, not from these reports)

  • Ask the seller for the battery's health or wear figure. Battery wear differs from unit to unit; the T490s owner reported 90% on theirs.
  • Ask whether a BIOS/supervisor password is set. Former business laptops sometimes arrive locked.
  • Check that the listing names the exact model and CPU.
