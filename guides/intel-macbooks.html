Old Intel MacBooks on Omarchy: what owners report

These guides collect public owner reports. We haven't tested these machines ourselves. "Unknown" means nobody has reported on that part yet, not that it fails. A "works" report means one owner said it worked for them, not that every feature is confirmed. Reports are dated; newer releases can change results. Last reviewed: Nov 8, 2026.

Reading these reports

  • The 2016 Touch Bar owner warns they skipped keeping Apple's EFI partition; read the install notes before wiping the drive.
  • We have no reports for other Intel years, including the 2017–2020 models. Nothing here covers them.
  • Apple silicon Macs are separate and use Omarchy's newer native support: see the M1 MacBook Air and M1 MacBook Pro pages.

Checks before buying a used Intel Mac (Apple's documentation)

  • Ask whether a firmware password is set. On an Intel Mac it blocks starting up from any disk other than the chosen startup disk, and Apple removes a forgotten one only with proof of purchase (Apple: Set a firmware password on your Mac).
  • Ask the seller to follow Apple's steps for preparing a Mac for sale, including signing out and erasing it (Apple: What to do before you sell your Mac). Apple's Activation Lock applies to Macs with Apple silicon or the T2 chip (Apple: Activation Lock for Mac).
  • Confirm the exact year and screen size from the model, not the listing title. One owner here wrote "I think it's from 2016."
