HP EliteBook 860 G9 and 850 (15-16 inch)

Setup: Omarchy (3.x then Omarchy 4 for the 860 G9 owner; version not stated for the 850) · Overall: Works

HP's larger EliteBooks (the 15.6-inch 850 and 16-inch 860) turn up cheap on the used market, and the two owners we found say Omarchy runs well on them. A used 860 G9 owner says getting into the BIOS to turn off Secure Boot took longer than installing Omarchy from USB, and Wi-Fi and the other drivers worked out of the box. He went back to Windows 11 for a while because he was struggling with Hyprland's tiling window manager, and found Windows harder to set up (he had to fetch Wi-Fi drivers by hand). When he reinstalled with Omarchy 4 it took under two minutes, and the fingerprint reader now works out of the box. He had one problem with a USB-C monitor in a dual-monitor setup, which he thinks came from changing screen scaling several times; his AI agent fixed it in about a minute and a half (he doesn't say what the fix was). A second owner says Omarchy is great on both his 14-inch EliteBook 840 and his 15.6-inch 850 and prefers the wider screen for tiling; he doesn't give the 850's generation. Suspend, webcam, audio and battery life aren't described in these reports, so they are unknown.
