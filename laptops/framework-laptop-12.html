Framework Laptop 12 (13th Gen Intel, 2-in-1)

Setup: Omarchy · Overall: Works (touchpad quirk)

Several owners run Omarchy on Framework's small 12-inch convertible and like it. One owner says it runs perfectly apart from screen rotation, which he fixed with his own add-on, and reports very low standby drain of about 0.4 to 0.6% per hour; another owner installed it in August 2026 and was still using it in mid November; and a Reddit user says it works well but suggests shrinking Hyprland's window margins because the screen is small. Another user found tiling a bit cramped on the small screen but saw no major bugs. The one recurring problem is the touchpad: two owners (one says he is on Omarchy's 'Quattro' release) found the pointer vanished or would only move down and to the right after install, some cold boots, or waking from hibernation, and a third saw a similar problem after resuming from suspend. A reboot clears it, and both detailed reports work around it by re-binding the touchpad driver, which one of them runs automatically at boot and on resume. Omarchy itself does not auto-rotate the screen or show an on-screen keyboard in tablet mode; owners use third-party add-ons (FW12Rotate, Gimbal) for that. Owners report on 13th Gen Intel models (i3-1315U, i5-1334U); nobody here reports on the newer board yet. Wi-Fi, webcam and speakers weren't covered in these reports, so they are unknown.
