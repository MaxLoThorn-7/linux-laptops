Dell XPS 13 (2026)

Setup: Omarchy · Overall: Works

Three owners on X say Omarchy runs well on a newly released Dell XPS 13. One names the 2026 Wildcat Lake model with 16GB of RAM and calls it really nice. Another says everything, including the power states, just works and uses it as a light client laptop. A third runs it next to a desktop and says the XPS 13 is so snappy they barely want the desktop. None of them say which components they tested, so Wi-Fi, camera, audio, display and battery life are still unreported in detail. Only one post names the Wildcat Lake chip, so the other two are assumed to be the same new XPS 13 but don't say so.
