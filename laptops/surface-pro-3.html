Microsoft Surface Pro 3 (2014)

Setup: Omarchy · Overall: Works

One owner on X dug a 2014 Surface Pro 3 out of a cabinet and installed Omarchy on it. They call it a straightforward install with no blockers, and say the touchscreen just works: tapping through panels and switching workspaces with a finger, with no configuration. They describe it as smooth and also say location (GPS) works. Their one complaint was software, not hardware: Synology Drive has no on-demand sync on Linux. They said battery testing was next and haven't reported on it yet. Wi-Fi, suspend, camera, audio, the Type Cover keyboard and the pen are all still unreported.
