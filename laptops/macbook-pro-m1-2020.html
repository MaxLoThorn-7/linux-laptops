Apple MacBook Pro 13" (M1, 2020)

Setup: Omarchy 4.5 (native M1) · Overall: Works (minor fix)

Four owners on X report Omarchy on an M1 MacBook Pro (the 2020 13-inch with Touch Bar). One, an ex-Apple employee, says Wi-Fi (including on a plane), speakers, Apple Music and AirDrop all work, calls it super fast, and earlier showed Touch ID working after some patching; they also say a Studio Display works and that Omarchy makes the Touch Bar useful. Another owner fixed the built-in mic, which showed up but recorded silence until they loaded the apple_sep driver in read-only mode so the Secure Enclave could pass audio. A third installed Omarchy 4.5 after native M1 support landed: the installer still had a few bugs but finished, and a fourth says an older M1 MacBook Pro runs very nicely. Suspend, camera and battery life are still unreported in detail. Screen size is taken from the 2020 M1 MacBook Pro line (13-inch with Touch Bar); owners say "M1 MacBook Pro" without always naming inches.
