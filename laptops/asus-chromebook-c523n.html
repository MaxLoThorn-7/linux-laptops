ASUS Chromebook C523N

Setup: Omarchy (installed after Debian, to fix partitions) · Overall: Works (minor fix)

One owner on X brought an old ASUS C523N Chromebook (8GB RAM) back to life with Omarchy. The Omarchy installer first failed with a “no space” error, so they installed Debian to sort out the disk partitions and then installed Omarchy over it. Audio needed configuring, which they had the Codex AI agent handle over SSH, and they don't say what the fix was. They also don't say whether they had to replace the Chromebook's firmware to boot Linux, which many Chromebooks need, so check that before you start. Wi-Fi, suspend, camera, display and battery are still unreported.
