Microsoft Surface Laptop 3 (2019)

Setup: Omarchy · Overall: Works (minor fix)

Two owners report Omarchy on a 2019 Surface Laptop 3. One on X says it runs perfectly fine, without naming which components they checked. Another on Reddit says it works well after installing the linux-surface kernel and applying a few tweaks so audio starts with the OS; they don't spell out those tweaks. A third Reddit commenter (same thread) says their 8GB Surface Laptop 3 runs great without the linux-surface kernel for anything they use, and they haven't tested the camera. Wi-Fi, suspend, camera, display, battery, Type Cover and pen are still unreported in detail; experiences with the Surface kernel appear to vary by owner.
