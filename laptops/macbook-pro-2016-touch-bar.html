Apple MacBook Pro 15" with Touch Bar (2016, Intel)

Setup: Omarchy · Overall: Works (minor fix)

One owner (posting in Italian) installed Omarchy on a 15-inch Touch Bar MacBook Pro they believe is from 2016 and had it up and running in about five minutes. Sound needed a fix: they say a firmware piece for Apple's T1 chip was missing and the speaker channel had to be enabled, which an AI coding agent sorted out for them. They praise the screen, and say it runs a little warm with everything maxed at 4K, which they suspect is dust inside. They also warn that they skipped the step of keeping Apple's EFI partition, so read the install notes first. A second owner says Omarchy made their 2016 MacBook Pro usable again, without naming the screen size. Wi-Fi, suspend, camera and battery are still unreported.
