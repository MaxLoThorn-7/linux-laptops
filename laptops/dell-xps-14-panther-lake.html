Dell XPS 14 (Panther Lake)

Setup: Omarchy 3.6 · Overall: Mixed (crash reports)

Several owners say Omarchy runs well on the new XPS 14: one installed it right out of the box, one ran World of Warcraft at 60 fps on medium settings, and one says fan noise is his only gripe. But one owner of a days-old XPS 14 reports two crashes in one morning and points to a Dell community thread about abrupt crashes and shutdowns on battery on the 2026 XPS 14 and 16. He doesn't say which Linux distro he runs (his earlier post mentions Ubuntu), and no fix has been reported here yet. Worth watching before you buy.
