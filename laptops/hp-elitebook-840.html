HP EliteBook 840 (G3, G4, G7 and G8 reports)

Setup: Omarchy (Omarchy 4 for one owner; version not stated by the others) · Overall: Works (fingerprint varies by generation)

HP's 14-inch business EliteBook 840 is a common cheap used laptop, and owners of several generations report Omarchy installing quickly and running well. A 2016 840 G3 installed in 5 minutes 27 seconds; an 840 G4 owner on Omarchy 4 says it feels three to four times faster than Windows 11 with no fan noise; an 840 G7 owner (10th-gen i7, 32GB RAM) says it is very fast, can charge over USB-C as well as from its standard power adapter, and that the fingerprint reader worked automatically; another G7 owner on Reddit got it working nicely; and an 840 G8 owner moved the same Omarchy install (442 days old) onto the machine along with the old SSD and RAM. One owner of both an 840 and a 15.6-inch 850 says Omarchy is great on both but prefers the wider screen for tiling. Fingerprint support differs by generation: an open Omarchy packaging issue says the G6 generation's Synaptics/Validity reader (USB 06cb:00b7, listed for the EliteBook 840 G6) has no driver in stock libfprint, so enrollment fails; the reporter got an unmerged upstream driver working on the closely related 745 G6, not an 840. On the 840 G4, one owner built a third-party fan-control add-on because HP's standard fan interface rejects fan commands on that board. Wi-Fi, suspend, webcam, audio, display and battery life aren't described in these reports, so they are unknown.
