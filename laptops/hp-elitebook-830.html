HP EliteBook 830 (G6, G8 and x360 830 G10 reports)

Setup: Omarchy (version not stated by these owners) · Overall: Works (fingerprint fix on G8)

HP's 13.3-inch EliteBook 830 is a common refurbished business laptop, and the owners who posted about it say Omarchy runs well. An 830 G6 owner (8th-gen Core i5, 16GB RAM) says that on Windows the battery died in about an hour, while on Omarchy it “goes for hours and doesn't go below 50%” and apps perform like new. An 830 G8 owner installed Omarchy first thing on a refurbished unit; the fingerprint reader didn't work at first, and he had an AI coding agent work on it for about an hour, after which the reader worked (he doesn't say what the fix was, so expect to do some work yourself). A convertible x360 830 G10 owner reports one problem: the external monitor shows “No signal” after idle or after unplugging and replugging USB-C; switching to another console and back restores it, but it keeps coming back, and updating Aquamarine (Hyprland's display backend) didn't fix it. Another 830 G8 owner tried Omarchy and went back to Ubuntu because he didn't like the tiling window manager, not because of a hardware problem. Wi-Fi, suspend, webcam, audio and the built-in display aren't described in these reports, so they are unknown.
