Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon (Gen 3 through Gen 13 Aura)

Setup: Omarchy (version not stated by these owners) · Overall: Works (fingerprint varies)

Seven owners on X posted about running Omarchy on a ThinkPad X1 Carbon between late October and mid November 2026, on generations from the 2015 Gen 3 up to the current Gen 13 Aura. All of them are happy with it. One owner uses a Gen 10 with 32GB of RAM, which he says cost him about $300, as his main machine; another installed it on a Gen 13 Aura in 12 seconds; a third revived a 2021 model with 32GB and an i7 that had sat unused because Windows got slow, and says it feels like new. Owners of a Gen 3 and a Gen 5 say their old machines have never felt so fast, and one person runs Omarchy on two X1 Carbons that he says work perfectly. The only problem reported is the fingerprint reader: on a Gen 3 the owner couldn't get it working (it didn't work for him under Ubuntu either), and on the 2021 model the fingerprint setup prompt threw an error on first boot until he ran 'omarchy update'. None of these owners said how Wi-Fi, sleep, the webcam, speakers or battery life behave, so those are unknown, and specific generations may differ. A cheap used Gen 9 or Gen 10 looks like a sensible pick from these reports, but check the exact generation before buying.
