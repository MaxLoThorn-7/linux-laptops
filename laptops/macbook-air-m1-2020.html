Apple MacBook Air (M1, 2020)

Setup: Omarchy 4.5 (native M1) · Overall: Works

Five owners on X report Omarchy on an M1 MacBook Air (the 2020 13-inch). One says installing it made the laptop feel like new again and calls it blazing fast. Another did a clean install of Omarchy 4.5 with the project's native macOS installer app after running an earlier Quattro alpha, and called the 4.5 install very polished; the same owner later said the internal microphone was selected but showed no input (including with Superwhisper), and they confirmed the mic still works in macOS. A third says a DMG install ran flawless out of the box, including audio, was super snappy, and is set up to dual-boot. A fourth says their M1 Air has a new life and calls Omarchy ridiculously smooth. A fifth says it's really cool on an MBA M1 and that connecting a USB-C to HDMI monitor (via something they call aurora) works. Wi-Fi, suspend, camera and battery life are still unreported in detail. The M1 MacBook Air was only sold as a 13-inch; owners say "M1 MacBook Air" without always naming inches.
