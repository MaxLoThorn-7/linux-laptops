Lenovo ThinkPad P14s (Gen 2 and other generations)

Setup: Omarchy (version not stated) · Overall: Works (wake quirk)

Several owners on X run Omarchy on a ThinkPad P14s. The most detailed is a Gen 2 owner who uses it full time with external displays, though in September he listed “unpredictable wake-up” as something he still needed to fix; he doesn't say whether his is the Intel or AMD version. Another owner of an AMD P14s (generation not stated) says the install was straightforward once Secure Boot was turned off, and that setting up the fingerprint reader let him skip typing his password. A third says battery and performance are excellent, and a fourth, who traded in a MacBook Air M3 for a P14s, says a few days in he could move his development work back onto the laptop. One Ryzen P14s owner reports one app-specific problem: YouTube live streams inside VRChat freeze after about 10 seconds. Wi-Fi, camera and audio aren't mentioned by anyone. Not an owner report, but useful: NixOS's hardware config for the Gen 2 AMD model says to set the firmware's Sleep State to “Linux” for suspend to work, which may be worth checking if wake-up misbehaves.
