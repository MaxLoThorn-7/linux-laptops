Lenovo Yoga (Intel/x86 models: Yoga 2 Pro, 2014 Yoga, Yoga 9 and others)

Setup: Omarchy (version not stated by these owners) · Overall: Works (Wi-Fi drops for one owner)

Lenovo's Yoga line covers many different laptops, so treat this as a family page: check the exact model in each report. Owners of older Intel Yogas say Omarchy runs well. One runs it on a 2014 Lenovo Yoga as a travel laptop and says it is “pretty crazy how well it runs”; another revived a 12-year-old Yoga 2 Pro with a new battery and a USB stick and calls Omarchy fast on old hardware (he set it up as his 9-year-old's coding machine). A Yoga 9 owner runs Omarchy on it and prefers keyboard-driven work to the touchscreen. The one problem report: an owner whose Yoga model isn't stated says the Wi-Fi keeps cutting out and has been surprisingly hard to fix. An older, now-closed Omarchy issue from another Yoga owner described Wi-Fi staying powered off after suspend; that reporter fixed it with a sleep hook that stops networking and reloads the Intel iwlwifi driver. Omarchy's own installer includes one Yoga-specific fix, a sound setting so both speakers and the bass work on the Yoga Pro 7 14IAH10. Snapdragon (ARM) Yogas such as the Yoga Slim 7x are a different case: one owner on Omarchy 4.5 says some things don't work yet. Suspend, webcam, audio on other models, display and battery life aren't described in these reports, so they are unknown.
