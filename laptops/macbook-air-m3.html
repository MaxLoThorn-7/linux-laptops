Apple MacBook Air (M3, 2024)

Setup: Omarchy 4.5 (native Apple Silicon; one owner used 4.5 RC3) · Overall: Works (minor fixes)

Omarchy 4.5 added native support for M-series MacBooks, and several M3 MacBook Air owners posted about it on X in early November 2026. Installs went smoothly for most of them: one owner says it “went really smoothly,” another says it runs well and the Mesa graphics driver works, though it lacks the polish of macOS. The two problems that came up more than once are external monitors and sleep. Two owners could not get an external monitor working over USB-C, and one owner on the 4.5 RC3 pre-release found sleep unreliable, sometimes not kicking in at all, and draining more power than macOS. A different owner says he got reliable sleep, AirDrop and AirPlay working with some AI-assisted fixes (for AirPlay he rebuilt a menu-bar plugin for ARM). A fourth owner reports that on the stock install of an M3 Air (model identifier Mac15,13) the brightness keys don't work because the display driver can't find the backlight; a custom kernel build fixes it but has to be rebuilt after every kernel update. No one in these reports said how Wi-Fi, the webcam, speakers or battery runtime compare, so those are unknown. Good for a tinkerer who mainly uses the built-in screen; check external-monitor status before relying on it.
