Dell Latitude 7350 Detachable (Intel, 2-in-1 tablet)

Setup: Omarchy (version not stated) plus the owner's published fix repos · Overall: Works (needs fixes)

One owner, Greg Yeutter, runs Omarchy on a Dell Latitude 7350 Detachable, a Surface-style Intel tablet with a keyboard cover, and he has published two public repos with everything he had to change. The biggest problem was sound: with the camera package Omarchy installs, the audio system stalled on the camera's raw devices and there was no sound at all until he disabled PipeWire's camera monitor, which means apps that go through PipeWire can't see the cameras (apps that open the camera directly still work). The built-in microphone could also lock up the sound card after suspend/resume; he fixed that by swapping in a newer kernel driver, which then passed his recording and suspend/resume tests. He also tamed volume buttons that jumped several steps per tap, set the high-resolution screen to exactly 2x, added finer low-brightness steps, and made the on-screen keyboard detect when the keyboard cover is attached. His separate tablet repo adds the on-screen keyboard, touch window moving and resizing, screen rotation, and a lock-screen PIN pad. Still open on his list: faint display artifacts in Chromium and an infrared camera that only works with an experimental driver. He doesn't say how Wi-Fi or battery life are, so those are unknown. This is more of a tinkerer's machine than a plug-and-play one, but every fix is written down.
